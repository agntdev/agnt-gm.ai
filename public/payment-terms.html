Bot deployment payments

Version: 12 September 2026

The price displayed in the app (currently 100 Telegram Stars) buys the initial launch of one bot. Failed deployment retries and subsequent redeploys of that bot do not require another deployment payment.

This is not a promise of unlimited AI generation or lifetime hosting. Any future paid improvement or hosting plan will be shown separately for your agreement before charging. Existing bots created before the billing rollout remain exempt from this launch fee.

We unlock deployment only after Telegram confirms your payment. If confirmation is delayed, do not pay again. A cancelled checkout does not unlock deployment.

Support and refunds

If your paid bot cannot be deployed, or you were charged incorrectly, contact payment support through the manager bot with /paysupport. Include the bot name and Telegram payment receipt. Never send passwords or bot tokens. We can investigate the charge and refund eligible failed or duplicate purchases in Telegram Stars to the original payer. Your statutory rights are unaffected.
